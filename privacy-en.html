MatomeDrop Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 21, 2026

💡 Quick Summary

MatomeDrop does not collect any personal data from users.
The App processes transfer preparation, history, and recommendations on-device. The developer cannot access the data or use it for advertising or tracking.

1. Overview

MatomeDrop (the “App”) helps people select photos, contacts, and documents to share with AirDrop. The developer does not collect personal data through the App. The App uses no developer-operated server, advertising SDK, analytics SDK, or tracking technology.

2. Data handled on your device and why

The App processes the photos, contacts, and documents you select, together with their display names, on-device references, and transfer history, to prepare transfers, repeat transfers, and make on-device recommendations. This information is processed and stored only on your device and is not accessible to the developer.

On iOS and iPadOS, the App uses Apple’s system pickers for photos, contacts, and documents. The App receives only the items you select and does not request access to your entire photo library or contact list.

On macOS, the App accesses Photos and Contacts after you grant permission so it can display items available to share. A contact vCard contains only the fields you select. The App handles only documents that you select or drag into the App.

3. Sharing and Apple system services

When you start sharing, Apple’s AirDrop feature sends the selected items to the recipient you choose. This is a user-initiated device-to-device transfer and is not data collection by the developer.

Apple’s privacy policy and your system settings apply to processing by Apple system services, including retrieving items stored only in iCloud Photos, device backups, AirDrop, and Siri or Spotlight suggestions. The App’s developer cannot access data processed by those Apple services.

Siri and Spotlight suggestions are off by default on macOS. If you enable them, the App provides macOS only with the transfer category (photo, document, or contact) and item count. It does not include contact names, phone numbers, addresses, filenames, or photo content.

4. Third parties, advertising, analytics, and tracking

The App does not send data to a developer-operated server or a third-party advertising or analytics service. It does not use data for advertising, create user profiles, or track people across apps or websites.

5. Retention and deletion

The Send List, on-device copies or references for selected items, and transfer history remain on your device until you delete them. In the App, choose Settings and then Delete Send List and History to remove the Send List, copies or references stored by the App, and transfer history. Your original photos, contacts, and documents are not deleted. Removing the App’s data with operating-system controls also removes the App’s on-device data.

6. Permissions and withdrawing consent

You can change or revoke Photos and Contacts permissions for the macOS App at any time in System Settings. Related selection and repeat-transfer features stop working after access is revoked. You can stop new Siri and Spotlight suggestions at any time in the App’s settings. On iOS and iPadOS, dismiss a system picker to cancel a selection.

7. Changes to this policy

If the App’s data practices change, the developer will update this policy and the App Privacy answers in App Store Connect. Material changes will be communicated in the App or on this public page.
