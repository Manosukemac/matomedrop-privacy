Privacy

Your data stays yours.

No account required. The developer does not collect your personal data.

FAQ

A few useful answers

Do I need an account?

No. You can use MatomeDrop without creating an account.

Can I choose which contact details to share?

Yes. Select the name, email address, or other details you want to include.

Can I reuse items from a previous transfer?

Restore past items to your send list to prepare another transfer. You may need to select an item again if the original is no longer available.

Is the Mac version available?

The Mac version is being prepared for release. Check the App Store for current availability.

Where can I get help?

Visit the support page to get in touch. Please avoid attaching photos, contacts, or documents that are not needed to explain the issue.
